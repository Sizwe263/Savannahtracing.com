NEXT-OF-KIN & HEIR TRACING

Trace next-of-kin, heirs and dependants across Namibia.

Estates, claims and benefit matters can stall when the correct next-of-kin, dependant or heir cannot be located. Savannah provides structured tracing and verification support so institutions can progress cases with stronger evidence.

Relationship context

Savannah records the relationship context and supporting information needed to distinguish credible family or dependant links from weak matches.

Identity verification

Names alone are not treated as proof. Identity details, dates of birth, known addresses, employers and other authorised evidence can support verification.

Institution-ready reporting

Savannah can document the tracing outcome and hand the verified case back to the responsible institution for its legal or benefit determination.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask about this service

Does Savannah decide who is legally entitled to an estate?

No. Savannah provides tracing and verification support; legal entitlement is determined by the responsible authority or institution.

Can Savannah trace dependants as well as heirs?

Yes. Savannah’s tracing scope includes beneficiaries, dependants, heirs and next-of-kin.

Is tracing limited to Windhoek?

No. Savannah supports tracing across Namibia.
