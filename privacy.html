Privacy

Savannah Tracing handles personal information for authorised tracing, verification, BenefitWatch monitoring, case administration, communications, security and lawful record-keeping.

Access to case information is restricted by role and business need. BenefitWatch does not collect banking details on the public website. Banking setup is completed through the approved secure onboarding and payment process, with Savannah retaining only the minimum mandate and payment metadata required for operations and compliance.

For privacy enquiries, contact info@savannahtracing.com.
