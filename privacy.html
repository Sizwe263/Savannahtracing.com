Privacy

Savannah Tracing handles personal information for authorised tracing, verification, BenefitWatch monitoring, case administration, communications, security and lawful record-keeping.

Access to case information is restricted by role and business need. BenefitWatch does not store the raw bank-account number submitted during the current onboarding flow; the backend stores a protected account fingerprint and mandate metadata pending integration with an approved debit-order provider.

For privacy enquiries, contact info@savannahtracing.com.
