UNCLAIMED BENEFITS NAMIBIA

A benefit may still be owed to you.

People can lose track of pension, retirement, insurance and related benefits after changing jobs, moving, changing contact details or when beneficiaries cannot be located. Savannah helps trace possible entitlements and manage verified cases toward resolution.

Common situations

A former employer may have provided a pension benefit that was never claimed, an insurer may need to locate a beneficiary, or an institution may hold a case that cannot progress because the member, dependant, heir or next-of-kin cannot be reached.

How Savannah helps

Savannah combines structured tracing, identity verification, relationship checks, supporting documentation and institutional follow-up. For BenefitWatch members, potential matches can move into a managed recovery workflow.

Start with what you know

Useful information can include previous employers, fund or policy references, former names, dates of birth, identity details and known institutions. Savannah only uses information and sources it is authorised to access.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask about this service

What are unclaimed benefits?

They are benefits or amounts that may remain unpaid because the rightful member or beneficiary has not completed a claim or could not be traced.

Can Savannah guarantee that I have an unclaimed benefit?

No. Savannah can trace and investigate possible entitlements, but final entitlement is determined by the responsible institution.

Can Savannah help with an old pension from a previous employer?

Yes, where sufficient information and lawful source access are available, Savannah can assist with tracing and case progression.
