Move a verified insurance benefit toward resolution.

Insurance benefits can remain unresolved when beneficiaries cannot be located, contact information is outdated or documents are incomplete. Savannah supports tracing, verification, documentation and managed follow-up with the responsible institution.

Find and verify

Manage the claim journey

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask about this service

Can Savannah approve an insurance claim?

No. Final claim and entitlement decisions remain with the responsible insurer or institution unless lawfully delegated.

What can Savannah manage?

Tracing, verification, documentation, institutional follow-up, case progression and payment confirmation support.

When is a case considered recovered?

When the responsible institution has confirmed and paid the relevant benefit through the managed case.
