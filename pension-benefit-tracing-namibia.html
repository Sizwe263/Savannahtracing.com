PENSION BENEFIT TRACING

Trace possible unpaid pension benefits in Namibia.

Former employees and beneficiaries can lose contact with retirement funds over time. Savannah helps investigate possible pension and retirement benefit cases using authorised information, identity verification and structured institutional follow-up.

Previous employment can matter

If you previously worked for an employer that provided a pension or retirement benefit, employment history, member numbers and fund references can be useful starting points.

Tracing before recovery

Savannah first establishes whether there is a credible match. A potential entitlement must then be verified with the responsible institution before any recovery case is progressed.

BenefitWatch monitoring

BenefitWatch members can be monitored over time across authorised and available sources rather than relying on a single one-off search.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask about this service

Can Savannah search for a pension from an old employer?

Yes, subject to sufficient identifying information and lawful access to relevant sources.

Who confirms whether money is actually payable?

The responsible pension or retirement fund, administrator or institution makes the final entitlement decision.

Does Savannah charge a recovery fee?

For BenefitWatch-managed recoveries, the agreed 10% Success Recovery Fee applies only after money is successfully recovered and payment is confirmed.
