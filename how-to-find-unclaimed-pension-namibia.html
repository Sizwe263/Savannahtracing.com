How to find a possible unclaimed pension in Namibia

If you changed employers, lost contact with a retirement fund or are unsure whether a pension benefit was ever paid, start by gathering the strongest identifying and employment information you have.

Start with employment history

List former employers, approximate employment dates, old staff numbers and any retirement or pension fund names you remember.

Gather identity details

Keep your full legal name, previous names, ID or passport number, date of birth and current contact details ready.

Look for old records

Payslips, benefit statements, policy documents, tax records and correspondence can help identify the responsible fund or administrator.

Verify before claiming

A possible match still needs to be verified with the responsible institution before entitlement or payment can be confirmed.

Use ongoing monitoring where useful

If no immediate match is found, BenefitWatch can continue checking authorised and available sources over time.

Frequently asked questions

Can Savannah confirm that a benefit definitely exists?

No. Savannah can investigate and verify possible matches, but the responsible institution makes the final entitlement decision.

Does Savannah collect banking details on these pages?

No. Banking and payment mandates are completed through the approved secure onboarding process.

Can institutions contact Savannah directly?

Yes. Institutions can contact Savannah for tracing, verification and case administration mandates.
