SAVANNAH BENEFITWATCH

Monitor possible unclaimed benefits for 3 people.

BenefitWatch is Savannah Tracing’s 12-month consumer membership for individuals and families in Namibia. It helps monitor possible unpaid pension, insurance and other financial benefits across authorised, participating and available institutional sources.

What BenefitWatch includes

One membership protects you plus two nominated people. Savannah manages tracing, verification, case progression and institutional follow-up when a credible potential entitlement is identified.

How the membership works

Founding members pay N$35 per month for 12 months. The standard price is N$49 per month. If Savannah successfully recovers money through a Savannah-managed case, the agreed 10% Success Recovery Fee applies only after payment is confirmed.

What BenefitWatch does not promise

BenefitWatch does not guarantee that a benefit exists and does not claim access to every pension fund, insurer or financial institution. Searches depend on lawful authority and the institutional sources available to Savannah.
